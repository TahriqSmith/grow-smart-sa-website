Why It's Different

The mathematics is never left to prediction

What generic AI does

  • Predicts the next likely token in a sequence, including for arithmetic, with no built-in way to check its own answer.
  • A plausible-looking but arithmetically wrong memorandum can reach a classroom before anyone notices.

What Grow Smart SA does

  • The underlying mathematics is computed and checked by code from a verified question template, not predicted by a language model.
  • AI is used only for wording and context, then an independent 30-year Mathematics teacher reviews the output before it ships.
