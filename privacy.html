Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Grow Smart SA (Pty) Ltd collects and uses personal information, through this website and through our training. It has not yet been reviewed by a legal adviser; that review is planned.

Who we are

Grow Smart SA (Pty) Ltd (registration number 2025/936856/07) trains South African educators to use AI in assessment and is developing an assessment app. Our website is https://growsmartsa.online. We are responsible for the personal information described here, except where we handle information for a school on its instruction (see Schools and learner information). Our Information Officer is Tahriq Smith, Director, registered with the Information Regulator (registration number 2026-070133): tahriq.smith@gmail.com, 082 801 6699, 67 Gladstone Road, Churchill Estate, Parow West, 7500.

What we collect

  • If you contact us: your name, email address, school or organisation, the topic you are interested in and your message.
  • If you take part in training: your name, contact details, school and subject, onboarding answers, attendance, the assessment papers and memoranda you upload, your feedback and recordings of sessions.
  • If you are a school or client contact: your name, role, email address and phone number, and the details on agreements and invoices.
  • If you take part in our research: your interview answers, recordings and notes, with your consent.
  • Payments: clients pay by EFT, so we see the payer's bank details on our statements. We do not collect card details.
  • Website: technical information such as your IP address, which our hosting provider records in server logs.

Why we use it

  • To answer enquiries and to deliver training: onboarding, attendance, assessments, feedback and certificates.
  • To invoice, keep business and tax records, and keep our systems secure.
  • To improve our training and products. Research uses your information only with your consent.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Sessions and recordings

Training sessions may be recorded so participants can revise, and recordings are shared only with the cohort. Our client agreements require notice at the start of each recorded session. We may use AI meeting-note tools (Google Gemini and Read AI) to produce summaries. Recordings and meeting notes are stored in our Google Drive, and only the Information Officer can open them, except for reviewers the Information Officer admits for a defined purpose, such as a due-diligence review. We use photos or recordings for marketing or reporting only with separate permission.

Lawful basis and consent

We process personal information only where POPIA gives us a lawful justification. Depending on the activity, this is a contract, your consent, a legal obligation or a legitimate interest in running our business. We explain the purpose when we collect the information. We do not assume that consent is the basis for everything.

Schools and learner information

Our training and software are for educators, and we do not ask for learner information. Our client agreements forbid uploading learner information to any AI tool. If a school asks us to mark scripts, we handle them only on that school's instruction, as its operator, and delete them when the job ends. The scripts we received were kept in a locked drawer and returned to the school after marking.

Who we share it with

  • The school or client you take part through, for its own participants.
  • Service providers: Google (Gmail, Drive, Meet, Classroom, Forms, Sheets), Read AI, WhatsApp, Vercel (website hosting), GoDaddy (domain) and Anthropic (the Claude AI we use for the assessment app).
  • Professional advisers such as an accountant or lawyer, when appointed, and authorities where the law requires.

We do not sell personal information. We send the AI provider assessment content, not personal information. This is true of every app run: only assessment content is sent.

Outside South Africa

Several of these providers store or process information outside South Africa. POPIA section 72 allows this where the recipient is bound by law or a binding agreement that gives equivalent protection, or where you consent. We are recording the basis for each provider in an internal register.

How we protect it

We limit access to the people who need it and keep personal information in restricted folders. We use two-step verification on our Google and code-hosting accounts and restrict sharing to named people.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires. Financial records are kept for the period tax and company law requires. We keep an internal retention schedule and delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Subject to the law, you may ask for access to your personal information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where we rely on it. We may need to verify your identity. Write to tahriq.smith@gmail.com. We will reply within the period the law allows and explain any lawful refusal or extension. You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa: https://inforegulator.org.za/

Cookies and website technology

Our website is hosted on Vercel and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. The contact form opens your own email app with a message addressed to us, so we receive only what you send.

Changes

We may update this notice as our services and practices change. The current version and its date are always on our website.
