The Problem

Assessment creation is unpaid overtime

A question paper and memorandum has to satisfy curriculum coverage, cognitive-level weighting, mark-allocation conventions and departmental moderation, all before a single learner writes it, and there's no protected time in a normal school day to do that work.

3.8 hrs
Average time to set and verify one CAPS-aligned paper & memo
2.5–6 hrs
Range reported across Western Cape educators interviewed
80%+
Share of surveyed teachers who split this work across evenings and weekends
~0 hrs
Protected admin time built into a normal school timetable

Because that work has nowhere to go but personal time, it becomes a direct driver of educator burnout, fatigue-induced grading inconsistencies and career attrition. Source: Grow Smart SA customer discovery interviews with Western Cape educators, 2026.

The Solution

Grow Smart SA closes the gap two connected ways

Training keeps us in direct, recurring contact with real teachers and real assessment problems, which shapes the product roadmap. The product is designed to eventually let teachers run that same workflow on their own, without a live session.
