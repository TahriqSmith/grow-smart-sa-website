Where We're Headed

A Western Cape beachhead, a national and adjacent path beyond it

1 · Beachhead (Now)

Public schools using the CAPS curriculum in the Western Cape, reached through direct school sales and SADTU-sponsored cohorts.

2 · National (SaaS expansion)

The 22,381 public schools across South Africa represent the long-term addressable market for the Grade 7–9 Mathematics subscription product once proven, with expansion to further subjects and grades.

3 · Adjacent (Horizontal)

TVET colleges and private higher education are a natural adjacent market, reachable through the founder's existing lecturing and DHET examination network once the schools product is proven.

A repeatable, profitable operating business rather than a single pilot: the training programme running across multiple schools, including sponsor-funded cohorts through partners such as SADTU, on a documented, delivery-ready playbook.

The Grade 7–9 Mathematics assessment tool live, generating recurring subscription revenue, and expanded to further subjects and grades based on validated teacher demand.

Grow Smart SA recognised as a trusted, curriculum-literate AI partner for South African schools.
